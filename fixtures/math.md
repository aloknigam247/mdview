## Mixed text + math

The Pythagorean identity $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$ is central to
trigonometry. The softmax function is

$$
\sigma(z)_i = \frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum_{j=1}^{K} e^{z_j}}
$$

which is used in classification models.
